Dead Route Privacy Policy

Dead Route is a solo and multiplayer game. You can play as a guest or use Google sign-in when it is available.

Google sign-in

Dead Route requests the OpenID and profile scopes. The sign-in service uses Google's account identifier to associate returning players with the same game account. Your Google profile name is used as your display name unless you set a name in the game. Other players can see your display name in online rooms. The browser uses a session cookie that expires after one hour. Standalone desktop pairing codes expire after five minutes. The service regularly clears expired sign-in data.

Game accounts and items

When account provisioning is enabled, the service uses the verified identifier on the server to derive a separate NEAR testnet account for the player. The test network can publicly show that account's address and transactions. Dead Route does not send your Google identifier or email to the test network. The service checks item ownership there to determine which items the game can unlock.

Game data and multiplayer

Game settings and progress are stored in the browser or on your device. If you join an online room, the room service relays game activity and your in-game name to the other players. If you enable voice chat, the game uses your microphone to send voice to nearby players in your match. Voice chat is off by default.

Your choices

You can play as a guest, sign out from the account screen, revoke Dead Route's access in your Google Account, and clear local game data through your browser or device settings. For privacy questions or requests about your data, email wisdomjesse9@gmail.com.
